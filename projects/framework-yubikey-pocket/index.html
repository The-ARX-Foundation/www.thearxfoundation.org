Framework YubiKey Pocket

Security Key Housing · Spec Sheet & Configurator
v1.0 — April 2026
Framework Laptop Compatibility: Framework 13" & 16" (identical expansion card slots), all current generations (Intel 11th–13th gen, AMD Ryzen 7040). USB-C expansion cards standard on all models. Expansion card slot: 55mm × 110mm × 4mm with recessed cavity behind.

Manufacturing Method
FDM 3D Print
PETG

Layer: 0.2mm (0.1mm pocket). Infill: 20–30%. Supports required. ~6–8hr print. Sand 220/400 grit + optional matte lacquer.

SLS 3D Print
Nylon PA12

Stronger than FDM. No supports needed. Excellent surface finish. 5–10 day lead via Shapeways or equiv.

CNC Machined
Aluminum 6061-T6

110×55×8mm blank. Face milling, pocket drilling, USB-C hole, rail grooves. Anodize Type II Class 2.
