§ 01 System Philosophy
Core Principle Design for full-time off-grid independence. Works for weeks without a dump station, handles two adults and a dog or two, and doesn't make the van smell like a porta-potty. Separating liquids from solids is the single most important decision — combined waste is what causes odor. Everything else follows from that.

Permanent build: composting/urine-diverting toilet (Nature's Head) as primary, backed by a Luggable Loo + WAG bags from REI for overflow, transit, and stealth. Dog waste runs its own track — outdoor dispersal on public land, bagged in town.

§ 01b Gray Water + Urine Dispersal
  1. BLM / dispersed federal land: Dilute and pour on ground, 200ft from water and camp. Urine is sterile and disperses safely. No bag, no waste — preferred method when boondocking.
  2. Urban / campground: Empty jug into any toilet, RV dump station, or pit toilet. Gas station bathrooms work — pour it down the toilet, rinse the jug.
  3. National forest / state parks: LNT rules — 200ft from water and high-use areas. Some alpine zones require pack-out even for urine; check area regs before going in.
  4. Gray water (dishes, cooking): Dr. Bronner's castile only (biodegradable — at REI). Scatter 200ft from water. Strain food solids out, pack those in trash. Small mesh strainer at the water station handles this every time.

§ 04 Dog Waste System
Key Rule Dog waste is high in pathogens — never bury it in a cathole like human waste. It doesn't decompose the same way and contaminates water sources. On public lands: bag and pack out, or move far from trails, water, and camp. In towns: always bag and bin.
