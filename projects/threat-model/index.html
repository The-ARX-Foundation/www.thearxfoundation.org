ARX Foundation // Classified

Sovereign Identity Threat Model

01 — threat surface

Password

Coercion, shoulder surf, replay

easy to break
Weakest link. Memorable = guessable, complex = written down. Rotate frequently. Use as decoy layer only — never sole gate.

NFC Card

Clone attack, relay, signal intercept

medium
Relay attacks are the real threat — amplify signal to clone a tap. Mitigated by challenge-response (card signs a nonce). Private key stays on card, never exported. Physical possession still required.

Static biometrics

Deepfake, mold, high-res photo

medium
Face, fingerprint, iris from photo — all cracked with enough resources. Iris from retinal blood vessel pattern is harder. On-device liveness detection raises the bar but doesn't seal it.

Wearable signal spoof

Replay recorded vitals, synthetic HRV

hard
Replaying recorded vitals fails because the baseline model expects real-time variation + contextual coherence. Synthetic generation is expensive and must fool a model trained only on you.

Semantic footprint

LLM mimicry, impersonation

hard → eroding
Style can be mimicked. Conceptual fingerprint — how you connect ideas, your specific reasoning leaps — is much harder. But LLMs are improving. Use as passive layer, not sole gate. Erodes over time.

ECG morphology

QRS waveform — unique per person

very hard
Not just heart rate — the shape of each beat is a biological fingerprint. Requires your actual heart. Cannot be replayed from a wearable relay. Medical-grade sensors needed; consumer chips are getting there.

Multi-factor combined

Coordinated simultaneous attack

extremely hard
Security compounds multiplicatively, not additively. Spoofing card + wearable + semantic footprint + ECG simultaneously under live physiological monitoring approaches impossible at current capability levels.

02 — biometric stack (live auth)

Temporal HRV + skin temp

Baseline-relative, not population-relative. Your calm at 2am ≠ your calm at 2pm. Continuous drift correction.

EDA (skin conductance)

Involuntary sweat response. Impossible to suppress under genuine duress. Pairs with HRV for duress fingerprint.

ECG morphology

QRS waveform shape. Requires your actual heart. Unspoofable by relay. Add when hardware matures.

Keystroke + gait dynamics

Passive. Always on. Dwell time, cadence, micro-movement from accelerometer. 20 years of research — robust.

Hidden layer (undisclosed)

Never reveal all layers. Attacker must spoof something they don't know exists. Roll new layers as old ones become known.

03 — duress response protocol

level 1

Subtle deviation

Silent flag. Extended monitoring. No action taken.

level 2

Moderate deviation

Require additional confirmation. Grace period before escalation.

level 3

Duress threshold

Crypto shred. Mesh alert fires. Assistance dispatched to trusted contacts.

forced ↩

Forced cancel

Biometrics still spiked during "cancel" → system acknowledges, silently escalates anyway.

04 — near-term unsolvable

Legal compulsion

Jurisdiction is the only partial answer. Cryptographic shredding helps — you can't hand over a key that doesn't exist.

Supply chain compromise

Hardware you didn't manufacture could be backdoored before it reaches you. Open hardware is the long play.

Trusted contact compromise

Your assistance network is as strong as its weakest member. Social graph is the actual attack surface.

Quantum

Breaks current asymmetric crypto. Timeline 10–15yr. Post-quantum primitives exist now — worth migrating early.

The cure is not a better lock. It's making the house not worth robbing. Every layer buys time for the larger transition — decentralized, post-scarcity infrastructure where aggregated personal data holds no extractable market value. The economic rational actor threat (~90% of attacks) dissolves. What remains is state-level and personal — a much smaller surface.
