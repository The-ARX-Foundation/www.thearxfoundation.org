80 characters
Script invented by Sultan Ibrahim Njoya of Cameroon (~1896), evolving through seven stages from pictographic to syllabic over 20 years. Glyphkeeper tracks the final stage — a 80-character syllabary. Extraordinary documented case of single-inventor script evolution. Unicode block: A6A0–A6FF.

Runic & Archaic Alphabets 2 scripts · ~95 characters

Pre-Latin European writing traditions. Characters carry phonetic, divinatory, and cosmological significance simultaneously. Glyphkeeper treats these as cognitive/perceptual tools as well as scripts.

24 runes · 3 aettir
The oldest runic alphabet, used c. 150–800 CE across Germanic Europe. Organized into three groups (aettir) of eight: Freyja's aett (ᚠ–ᚹ), Heimdall's aett (ᚺ–ᛃ), Tyr's aett (ᛏ–ᛟ). Each rune carries phonetic value, proto-name, and cosmological meaning. Physical moss agate set in use alongside Glyphkeeper digital module. 59 syllabic signs
Mycenaean Greek syllabary (~1450–1200 BCE), the oldest deciphered writing for any Greek-family language. Deciphered by Michael Ventris in 1952. Each sign represents a consonant-vowel pair. Circular & Fictional Writing Systems 2 systems · reference + structural study

Writing systems from constructed worlds — studied for their structural logic, aesthetics, and encoding philosophy. Each reveals something about the relationship between form and meaning. ARX treats these as design objects.

Nomai Writing (Outer Wilds)
Circular Systems — Procedural
Reference
◯ ◉ ◈ ⬡ ⬢ — Archimedean spiral encoding
Deterministic, parametric writing system. Each message always produces an identical Archimedean spiral. Characters map to positions along the spiral curve via integer encoding: consonant = top row, vowel = left column of a grid. Connection lines encode syntactic relationships between word-spirals. Handwriting mode adds controlled noise. Core structural insight: proceduralism as cultural expression — a deterministic civilization would write deterministically.

Circular Gallifreyan (Sherman's)
Circular Systems — Expressive
Reference
◉⊙○ — Word circles nested in sentence circles
Artistic writing system designed by Loren Sherman for Doctor Who fandom. Each word becomes a circle. Sentence circles contain word circles. Consonants defined by 4 base shapes (Ω, O, ⌢, ⊖) with dots and lines as modifiers; 5 vowels attach to consonants or float independently. Counter-clockwise reading direction. Rules are guides, not law — two writers produce visually distinct Gallifreyan from identical text. Core structural insight: aesthetic expression over prescription mirrors Time Lord philosophy of transcending rule-systems.

Expressive / artistic alphabet
